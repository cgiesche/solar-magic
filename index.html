What is Solar Magic?

Solar Magic is a level editor for Super Mario World on the Super Nintendo. Start from your copy of the game, change anything from a single block to whole worlds, and save the result as your own ROM hack to play on an emulator or share as a patch with friends.

Everything you see in the editor looks exactly like it will in the game, so there are no surprises when you press play.

What you can do

  • Design levels

    Place ground, pipes, enemies and power-ups, set up doors and pipes between areas, and paint backgrounds.

  • Music

    Add your own songs and listen to every level's music in the editor.

  • Message boxes

    Rewrite what the game's message blocks say.

Made for the SMW hacking community

Solar Magic works with the formats and tools the Super Mario World hacking scene has built over the years. The patches, blocks, sprites, music and graphics you find on SMW Central work as they are – no conversion, no command line.

  • Patches for Asar
  • UberASM code
  • Custom blocks for GPS
  • Custom sprites for PIXI
  • Music & sound effects for AddmusicK
  • Graphics: ExGFX, Map16, palettes
  • Lunar Magic level files (.mwl): backgrounds, tiles and colors
  • Callisto projects such as the Romhack Races baserom

What you make is a regular Super Mario World ROM: play it on any emulator or share it as a BPS patch.

Get started in three steps

  1. Start a new project. Choose a clean Super Mario World (US) ROM made from your own copy of the game.
  2. Make it yours. Pick a level and start building.
  3. Play and share. Test your changes, then save your ROM or a patch to share.

Questions

Do I need to install anything?
No. Solar Magic runs in any modern web browser. A desktop app is available as well.
Where do I get a ROM?
Solar Magic does not come with the game. You start from a clean Super Mario World (US) ROM that you made from your own copy.
Can I use patches, blocks and sprites from SMW Central?
Yes. Drop the download into the Packages dialog: patches, UberASM code, blocks, sprites, music and graphics are installed together.
Is my ROM uploaded anywhere?
No. Your ROM and your work stay on your own computer. Nothing is sent to a server.
I know Lunar Magic. Will I feel at home?
Yes. Solar Magic covers what you know from Lunar Magic, uses its file formats and adds things like play-testing right inside the editor.
