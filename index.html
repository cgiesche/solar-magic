Solar Magic

The Super Mario World level editor that runs in your browser.

Build your own levels, reshape the overworld, paint new graphics and play your creation the moment you made it – free and without installing anything.

Or simply drop your Super Mario World ROM or project file onto this page.

What is Solar Magic?

Solar Magic is a level editor for Super Mario World on the Super Nintendo. Open your copy of the game, change anything from a single block to whole worlds, and save the result as your own ROM hack to play on an emulator or share as a patch with friends.

Everything you see in the editor looks exactly like it will in the game, so there are no surprises when you press play.

What you can do

  • Design levels

    Place ground, pipes, enemies and power-ups, set up doors and pipes between areas, and paint backgrounds.

  • Edit the overworld

    Reshape the map, connect your levels and decide where every path leads.

  • Play right away

    Try your level instantly inside the editor – with music and sound effects.

  • Graphics & colors

    Repaint tiles, change palettes and bring things to life with animations.

  • Music

    Add your own songs and listen to every level's music in the editor.

  • Custom content

    Use custom blocks, sprites and patches made by the Super Mario World hacking community.

Get started in three steps

  1. Open your ROM. Choose a Super Mario World (US) ROM made from your own copy of the game.
  2. Make it yours. Pick a level and start building.
  3. Play and share. Test your changes, then save your ROM or a patch to share.

Questions

Do I need to install anything?
No. Solar Magic runs in any modern web browser. A desktop app is available as well.
Where do I get a ROM?
Solar Magic does not come with the game. You open a Super Mario World (US) ROM that you made from your own copy.
Is my ROM uploaded anywhere?
No. Your ROM and your work stay on your own computer. Nothing is sent to a server.
I know Lunar Magic. Will I feel at home?
Yes. Solar Magic covers what you know from Lunar Magic and adds things like play-testing right inside the editor.
