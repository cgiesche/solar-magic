Solar Magic

The Super Mario World level editor that runs in your browser.

Build levels, reshape the overworld, paint graphics and play your creation the moment you made it – with the patches, blocks, sprites and music the community has made over the years.

A new project starts from a clean Super Mario World (US) ROM. You can also drop the ROM or a project file onto this page.

  • Free
  • Nothing to install
  • Your ROM never leaves your computer
  • Desktop app for Windows, macOS & Linux

Features

Everything you need to make a hack

What you see in the editor is what the game shows: Solar Magic draws your levels with the game itself, so there are no surprises when you press play.

Levels and the overworld

Place ground, pipes, enemies and power-ups, connect areas with doors and pipes, and paint backgrounds. On the overworld, reshape the map, link your levels and decide which paths each exit opens.

  • Objects, sprites, entrances and exits, layer 2 and layer 3
  • Overworld paths, events and the levels behind each tile
  • Undo and redo, and one search across objects, tiles and sprites

Play right away

Press T and you are in your level – with music and sound effects, a keyboard or a gamepad. No emulator to start, no file to save first.

  • Meters for slowdown and sprite slots while you play
  • See where Mario died and what killed him
  • Save states to try a jump again and again

Graphics, colors and animation

Repaint tiles pixel by pixel, build your own blocks from them, change palettes and bring things to life with animations – while a preview shows the level as you change it.

  • Map16 tiles with what they act like and where they are used
  • ExGFX, palettes and ExAnimation
  • Import PNG images and Lunar Magic graphics files

  • Music

    Add your own AddmusicK songs and listen to every level's music in the editor.

  • Message boxes

    Rewrite what the game's message blocks say, in the game's own font.

  • Share your hack

    Export a playable ROM for any emulator, or a BPS patch to share.

Community content

Made for what the SMW community built

Solar Magic speaks the formats of the Super Mario World hacking scene. Downloads from SMW Central work as they are: drop one into the editor and everything it contains is installed in one go – and removed again just as easily.

  • Asarpatches
  • UberASM Toollevel, game mode and global code
  • GPScustom blocks
  • PIXIcustom sprites
  • AddmusicKsongs and sound effects
  • Callistowhole projects, like the Romhack Races baserom
  • Lunar Magic formatsMap16, ExGFX, palettes, level files (.mwl)
  • BPSpatches of your hack to share

FAQ

Questions

Do I need to install anything?
No. Solar Magic runs in any modern web browser. A desktop app is available as well.
Where do I get a ROM?
Solar Magic does not come with the game. You start from a clean Super Mario World (US) ROM that you made from your own copy.
Can I use patches, blocks and sprites from SMW Central?
Yes. Drop the download into the Packages dialog: patches, UberASM code, blocks, sprites, music and graphics are installed together.
Is my ROM uploaded anywhere?
No. Your ROM and your work stay on your own computer. Nothing is sent to a server.
I know Lunar Magic. Will I feel at home?
Yes. Solar Magic covers what you know from Lunar Magic, uses its file formats and adds things like play-testing right inside the editor.
