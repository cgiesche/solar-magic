Whose work Solar Magic builds on

Solar Magic exists because of two decades of work by the Super Mario World hacking community. This page names the tools and libraries the editor contains, the tools and formats it follows, the documentation it was written from and the resources it was tested with.

Solar Magic is an unofficial fan project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Nintendo. Super Mario World and Nintendo are trademarks of Nintendo. The editor does not come with the game: it works on a ROM you supply yourself.

Tools and formats the editor follows

Solar Magic reads and writes what these tools defined, so that the community's resources work in it and its ROMs work in theirs. No code is taken from them unless said.

Lunar Magic
The editor every Super Mario World hack is built with, and the measure for this one. Solar Magic uses its file formats (.mwl, .map16, ExGFX, palettes, object side files), its layout of levels, the overworld, messages, level names and ExAnimation in the ROM, and its names for objects. A few of its routines are installed in the ROM byte for byte, so that tools and patches written for a Lunar Magic ROM find them.
FuSoYa
PIXI
The format and interface of custom sprites and the names and contracts of their shared routines.
JackTheSpades, RPGHacker, Tattletale, Atari2.0; contributions by KevinM, tim-burget, dtothefourth, Super Maks 64, randomdude999, akaginite, Lx5, spooonsss, Fernap and others; routines after Akaginite, imamelia, MarioE and GIEPY; cluster sprites by Alcaro, extended sprites by imamelia
GPS Gopher Popcorn Stew
The format of custom blocks and the names and contracts of their standard routines.
p4plus2; maintained by TheBiob, GHB and Arinsu; contributions by Majorflare, MarioFanGamer and Vitor Vilela; routines after Akaginite, Tattletale and imamelia
UberASM Tool
The format and calling conventions of UberASM code.
Vitor Vilela, Fernap and others
Callisto
Callisto projects as packages: its configuration, its exports and the defines it passes to patches.
Underrout
ObjecTool
Custom objects with extra bytes, and custom extended objects.
imamelia; the original ObjecTool by 0x400
UberASM Objects System
Objects that set a flag when the level loads: their names and tooltips.
AmperSam, underway, MarioFanGamer
Dynamic Levels
The idea of horizontal levels with another height, as built into Lunar Magic 3.00.
Vitor Vilela
Patches the editor recognizes
The line-guide “acts like” fix, the level graphics loading optimization and the VRAM patch optimization: the editor does what they do and tells you when a package brings them.
imamelia and worldpeace; freeplay; KevinM
Aseprite
How the pixel editor paints: its buttons, keys and tools. No code.
Igara Studio, David Capello and contributors
